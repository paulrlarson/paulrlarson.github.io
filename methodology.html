Auditable Geopolitical & Institutional Sensemaking

How each assessment in the series is built.

Watch the briefings

Most conflict and unrest commentary relies on pundit intuition, single-narrative wire summaries, or unverified AI summaries. Every assessment published in my public series is built on a structured, source-auditable analytical workflow grounded in rigorous open-source tradecraft, institutional benchmarking, and empirical conflict-resolution research. For each event, multi-language regional reporting, official statements, and international monitoring data are logged at the atomic claim level, strictly separating stated positions, dated actions, and underlying institutional constraints. Candidate explanations are evaluated against peer-reviewed causal mechanisms and historical precedents, followed by independent line-by-line source verification passes so every factual statement traces directly to an opened source.
