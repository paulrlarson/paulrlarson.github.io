Executive Profile

Institutional Strategy, Operational Transformation & Conflict Sensemaking

Profile

Across a 15-year career spanning Amazon Web Services (AWS), the U.S. Department of State, and field engagements across a dozen countries, Paul Larson enhances operational effectiveness by identifying opportunities for improvement, designing data-driven solutions, and working across disciplines to implement them. Combining a master's degree in Conflict Analysis and Resolution with credentials in Lean Six Sigma (Black Belt), Project Management (PMP), and Enterprise Risk Management, he guides organizations through complex transformations, reducing operational cost while improving performance, risk visibility, and stakeholder engagement.

Whether scaling AI and collaboration workflows in global deal strategy at AWS, leading the State Department's internal management innovation team, facilitating scenario planning and operational strategy at diplomatic missions worldwide, or evaluating foreign assistance programs abroad, his work centers on four core capabilities:

Four core capabilities

Operational Transformation & Innovation Leadership

At the U.S. Department of State, designed and led a four-person internal innovation team applying a phased problem-solving lifecycle to enterprise-wide operational challenges. Coordinated the 90-day Reimagine Task Force (overseeing a $3.2M consulting engagement), launching 26 initiatives across technology, automation, HR policy, and change management. Translated ambiguous remote-work policies into measurable criteria across 14,230 positions, increasing majority-telework eligibility from 23% to 49%.

AI Adoption & Auditable Research Systems

As Senior Program Manager in AWS Global Deal Strategy & Programs, drove 100% installation and training of new AI desktop tools during an accelerated sprint (outperforming peer teams at 49% to 90%) and achieved 100% collaboration-platform migration with 0% dissatisfaction. Independently engineered a structured, source-auditable open-source research and conflict-analysis workflow that pairs multi-language primary reporting with multidisciplinary academic frameworks and claim-level verification.

Enterprise Risk & Analytics Product Ownership

Relaunched the State Department's global Tableau Management Analytics Portal, integrating service speed, workload, staffing, and satisfaction indicators to grow new users by 93% in three months. Earlier, designed a bureau-wide foreign assistance framework at State (INL) integrating risk management, program management, and quantitative performance dashboards.

Global Operations, Stabilization & Field Facilitation

Designed and led scalable workshops at U.S. diplomatic missions worldwide to improve management service delivery and align interagency operations with geopolitical shifts. Highlights include assessing 200+ services to generate 60 process improvements in Hong Kong, leading geopolitical scenario planning in Indonesia, and standing up provincial business hubs and foreign-assistance evaluations on the ground in Kabul, Afghanistan.
