Executive Profile

Profile

Across 13+ years in global diplomatic operations, risk architecture, and enterprise technology, Paul Larson helps leaders turn ambiguous institutional problems into clear decision frameworks, analytics products, and adopted operating models. His work sits at the intersection of geopolitical and operational risk prioritization, public-sector innovation, and decision support: diagnosing why complex systems stall, building the analytical model or dashboard that shows where to act first, and guiding cross-functional teams through adoption.

At the U.S. Department of State, he built and led the internal management innovation team, coordinated the Under Secretary's 90-day Reimagine Task Force ($29M portfolio across 26 technology, automation, and workforce initiatives), scaled a policy-assessment model across 14,230 direct-hire positions, and relaunched a global Tableau operational analytics portal (+93% new users in three months). To align diplomatic resources with shifting foreign-policy risks, he co-designed an enterprise staffing prioritization model that reallocated 95 Foreign Service positions from Iraq and Afghanistan toward priority posts in Asia, led geopolitical scenario planning and operational diagnostics across seven overseas missions, architected a global criminal-threat and institutional-capacity framework for U.S. rule-of-law foreign assistance, and stood up conflict-zone program evaluations in Kabul, Afghanistan.

At Amazon Web Services (AWS), he designs cross-functional deal-risk governance mechanisms and AI-assisted evaluation workflows for global enterprise teams. He holds an MS in Conflict Analysis and Resolution from George Mason University, a Phi Beta Kappa BA in Politics and International Relations from Drake University, and credentials in Project Management (PMP), Lean Six Sigma (Black Belt), and Enterprise Risk Management in Government (George Washington University).
