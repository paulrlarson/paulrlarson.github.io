Executive Profile

Profile

Over a 15-year career spanning Amazon Web Services (AWS), the U.S. Department of State, and international field engagements, Paul Larson enhances operational effectiveness by identifying opportunities for improvement, designing data-driven solutions, and working across disciplines to implement them. Combining a master's degree in Conflict Analysis and Resolution with credentials in Lean Six Sigma (Black Belt), Project Management (PMP), and Enterprise Risk Management, he guides organizations through complex transformations, reducing operational cost while improving performance, risk visibility, and stakeholder engagement.

His work centers on three focus areas: geopolitical and conflict risk, operational transformation, and analysis and decision support.
